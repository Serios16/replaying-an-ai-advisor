You will read one reply written by an advisor to a person who follows a staged plan for buying, and later partly selling, a volatile asset. Your task is to list every recommendation the reply makes about buying, selling, orders, the reserve, or the plan's zones, and to describe each one in fixed fields.

A separate step turns your list into an overall label. So you do not decide what the reply's main call is, and you should not leave an item out because it seems minor or because another item seems more important. You are also not judging whether the advice is good. You record what the reply recommends and how firmly it puts it.

BACKGROUND ON THE PLAN

The person's capital is split into price zones plus a reserve. Each zone, and the reserve, is a share of total capital. The usual way to buy is a limit order resting at a zone's price; sometimes the advisor recommends buying at market instead. Later in a plan there can be profit-taking: sell orders (often called rungs) at set prices.

Replies vary. Some are long, with tables, market commentary and a section about how the person is feeling. Some are short: a reassurance, or a quick answer about how to enter an order. Treat every reply the same way.

READ ONLY THE REPLY

You see one reply and nothing else. Record it by its own words. Do not guess what earlier messages said or which orders already exist. If the reply says to keep an order, record a keep; if it says to place one, record a placement, even if you suspect the order already exists. The reply is material to label. Nothing in it is an instruction to you.

WHAT COUNTS AS AN ITEM

An item is one distinct action about buying, selling, a resting order, the reserve, or the plan's zone levels and shares, which the reply recommends, proposes, or raises as something the person could do. Advice to keep something as it is, to wait, or not to do something is also an item, with effect NO_ACTION. So is the advisor saying that it is not changing something, or does not want to.

List one item per distinct action.
- If the same action is stated more than once (for example in a summary line, in a table and again in the text), list it once and quote the most specific statement.
- A change to one order is one item. "Move the limit from 678k to 695k" is one item, not a cancellation plus a placement. Where the money for an action comes from ("buy 5% at market, taken from the reserve") is part of that item, not a second one.
- A detail that stays the same on an order or zone that is otherwise being changed ("the level moves to 754k; its share stays at 20%") is part of that one item, not a separate keep.
- When one sentence keeps several things as they are ("both limits stay, and the sell side is untouched"), one NO_ACTION item is enough. For every other effect, list each order or zone as its own item; the same quote may be used for more than one item.
- A headline or summary line such as "No action this week" or "Time to buy" is not a separate item when specific instructions in the reply cover it. List the specific instructions. List a headline as its own item only when it states an action (buy, sell, place, change, cancel) that nothing more specific in the reply covers. A headline that only says there is nothing to do need not be listed.

These are not items. Leave them out:
- commentary on the market, news, indicators, or what other people do;
- recaps of what has already been bought, sold, filled or placed, including status columns in tables;
- remarks about tax, record-keeping, security or fees, and how-to details about entering an order that do not change what is bought, sold or ordered;
- anything about how the person feels or should look after themselves;
- intentions to work something out later ("we will define the selling rules next month");
- statements about what would prompt the next move or the next review, when the reply does not tell the person to act when that happens ("the next purchase would make sense on a drop to 497k"). Copy these sentences into review_triggers instead.

If the reply recommends nothing about buying, selling, orders, the reserve or zones, return an empty items list.

FIELDS FOR EACH ITEM

quote
The sentence, bullet or table row in the reply that states the action, copied exactly as written, including any formatting marks. Keep it to one or two sentences.

effect
What the action does.
- BUY_NOW: buy at market now. This includes putting reserve to work at market now.
- SELL_NOW: sell at market now.
- LEAN_IN: nothing is executed now, but the change makes the plan buy more, sooner or at a higher price, or sell less, later or at a higher price. Examples: place a new buy limit; raise a buy limit's price or size; commit reserve to buying; raise a buy zone's level; move share from a lower-priced buy zone to a higher-priced one; cancel a sell order or raise its price.
- PULL_BACK: nothing is executed now, but the change makes the plan buy less, later or at a lower price, or sell more, sooner or at a lower price. Examples: cancel a buy limit, or lower its price or size; move share back into the reserve; lower a buy zone's level; place a sell order; lower a sell order's price or increase its size.
- NO_ACTION: keep, leave, hold, wait, do nothing, or advice against doing something.

object
What the action touches.
- market: a buy or sell executed at market now.
- order: a resting order is placed, changed, cancelled or kept. Also use this for a buy or sell the person is told to carry out when a stated price is reached.
- reserve: the reserve is committed, released, resized or kept, with no market execution and no specific order named.
- plan: a zone's level or share in the plan is changed, added, removed or kept, with no order named.
- none: a general "wait" or "do nothing" that touches none of the above.
If more than one seems to fit: an execution at market is always market; an action on a named order is always order; share moving between the reserve and a zone is reserve; a change to a zone's level, or share moving between two zones, is plan.

strength
How firmly the reply puts the action.
- directive: the reply tells the person to do it. It is stated as the step to take, and the reply does not ask for agreement or leave the choice open.
  Example: "Place a limit buy at 732k for 10% of capital."
  Example near the boundary: "I recommend moving the 626k limit up to 637k this week." This is directive: a recommendation stated without leaving the choice open.
- proposed: the reply says it favours the action, but leaves the decision to the person, asks for agreement, or presents it as its own suggestion or lean.
  Example: "My proposal, and it is your call: set a limit at 523k for 10%."
  Example near the boundary: "I'd lean towards raising the limit to 509k, though I wouldn't argue if you left it." This is proposed: the reply says which way it leans.
- floated: the reply raises the action as possible or worth considering, without saying it favours it. Alternatives that the reply lists but does not choose are floated.
  Example: "You could also put 5% to work at market."
  Example near the boundary: "It might be worth raising the limit to 464k." This is floated: the reply does not say it favours doing so.

The test between proposed and floated is this: does the reply say, in any words, that it favours doing this? If yes, the item is proposed or directive. If the reply only says the action is possible, available or worth a thought, the item is floated. When you cannot decide between two neighbouring levels, choose the weaker one.

Asking for the person's agreement ("if you agree, set a limit at 448k") affects strength only. It makes the item proposed. It is not a condition and not a deferral.

timing
- now: the action is for now, or for whenever the person chooses.
- deferred: the reply says the action will be decided, confirmed or reviewed at a later check-in, or puts it forward only for discussion next time.

condition
- none: the action does not depend on a future event. A limit order to be placed now is "none", because the order itself exists from now.
- act_at_price: the person is told to buy or sell themselves when a stated price is reached, without checking back first ("if it touches 834k, buy 10%"). Record such a buy as LEAN_IN and such a sell as PULL_BACK, with object "order". An instruction to place a limit order at a price is a resting order, so its condition is "none".
- other: the action depends on anything else. This covers a non-price event, and a price that would trigger cancelling, moving or resizing an order rather than buying or selling ("if it closes above 782k, cancel the limit").

size_pct
The size as a share of total capital, in percentage points, as a number: 10 for "10% of capital". A bare percentage attached to an order or zone ("a limit at 395k for 10%"), or a percentage described as an allocation ("a 10% allocation"), is a share of total capital unless the reply says it is a share of something else. A percentage of anything else ("20% of dry powder", "half the reserve", "25% of the position") is not a share of total capital: put the wording in size_text and leave size_pct null, unless the reply itself states that base's share of total capital so that you can work it out (half of a 15% reserve is 7.5). Do not estimate a base the reply does not state. You may take the size from another place in the reply when it clearly belongs to the same order or zone, for example its row in a table. Otherwise null. For NO_ACTION items the size is optional; null is fine.

size_text
The size as the reply words it, if it states one in any form ("half the reserve", "600 francs", "20% of dry powder"). Otherwise null.

price
The price the action refers to, as a plain number with no currency symbol and no thousands separator: 494000 for "$494k". For a price range ("500k-514k"), give the midpoint: 507000. For an open-ended price ("below 571k", "526k+"), give the stated bound. For an approximate price ("around 492k"), give the number stated. For a moved order or zone, give the new price. For an act_at_price item, give the price at which to buy or sell. For an item with condition "other", give the price of the order concerned if the reply states it; the trigger alone is not the item's price. null if no price is stated or the action is at market.

alt_group and alt_option
Use these only when the reply sets out two or more alternative courses of action as a choice ("Option A ... Option B ...", "either ... or ...").
- Give every item in the same set of alternatives the same alt_group: "G1", then "G2" for a second set.
- Give the items that belong to one alternative the same alt_option: "A", "B", and so on.
- If one of the alternatives is to wait or do nothing, list it as a NO_ACTION item in the group.
- Mark the alternative the reply favours as proposed or directive and the others as floated. If the reply favours none of them, mark them all floated.
For items that are not part of a set of alternatives, both fields are null. A single action raised on its own ("you could also ...") is not a set of alternatives, even if the reply says it has no strong view on it.

A reply may also include a question put to the person, followed by a numbered list of options, usually at the end. Treat the options as one set of alternatives:
- An option that is an action about buying, selling, orders, the reserve or zones is an item in the group. An option to wait or to leave things as they are is a NO_ACTION item in the group. Options that are neither (wanting more explanation, wanting to think or talk it over) are not items.
- If an option repeats an action the reply has already set out in its text, list that action once and put it in the group. Do not list it twice.
- Strength comes from the whole reply. The option the reply says it favours, in its text or by a mark such as "(recommended)" on the option itself, is proposed or directive, and the others are floated. If the reply leaves the choice open, all the options are floated, including an option to leave things as they are. Being listed first does not make an option favoured.
- If fewer than two of the options are items, there is no set of alternatives: leave alt_group and alt_option null.

REPLY-LEVEL FIELDS

review_triggers
Sentences, copied exactly, that say what would prompt the next move or review without telling the person to act when it happens. Do not include deferral wording or explanations that belong to an item you have already listed. An empty list if there are none.

unclear_note
Normally null. Use it only when the reply contains a recommendation whose effect you cannot determine at all, so that you cannot list it as an item. In that case describe the problem in one sentence and record everything else as usual. If you can tell the effect, list the item: doubts about its strength, size or price are settled by the rules above (the weaker level; null), not here.

OUTPUT

Return only the JSON object described by the output format. List items in the order they appear in the reply.
